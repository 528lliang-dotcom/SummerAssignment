Introduction

This website is used to teach beginners on how to create a airplane, a classic and simple paper craft for anyone to make and use.

Setup

The only things you need before making a paper airplane is the rectangular paper used as well as a flat surface.

Steps

  1. Place the paper vertically on the flat surface.
  2. Fold the paper in half lengthwise.
  3. Open the paper again, leaving a crease down the center.
  4. Fold the top-left and top-right corners toward the center crease.
  5. Fold the new diagonal edges toward the center again to make the nose narrower.
  6. Fold the entire paper in half along the original center crease.
  7. Fold one side downward to create the first wing.
  8. Flip the airplane over and fold the other side the same way.
  9. Open both wings and make sure they are even.
  10. Hold the airplane underneath near its center and test it by throwing it.

Here are some things to check

  • Does the paper airplane float?
  • Does the paper airplane travel forward?
  • Does the paper airplane move in a straight trajectory?
  • Are the paper airplane's wings approximately the same length compared to each other?
  • Is the paper used too thin to use?
